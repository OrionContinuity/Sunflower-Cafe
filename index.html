The Kitchen02

A short walk from anywhere in town

Breakfast runs all day, lunch comes off the same griddle, and Friday belongs to the fish fry. Nothing on this page is a chain recipe.

Come hungry, sit a while, and ask what the soup is.

  • 1

    Breakfast all morning

    Omelets built to order, skillets under country gravy, benedicts, and a stack of Sunflower Pancakes with chocolate chips and bacon inside.

  • 2

    A kitchen with two accents

    Chilaquiles and huevos rancheros under homemade salsa sit on the same board as a broasted chicken sandwich and a turkey club.

  • 3

    Friday is the fish fry

    Baked or fried, by the plate or by the bucket for the whole table — and the kitchen stays open until eight instead of three.

  • 4

    Desserts made here

    Apple dutch pie, triple threat chocolate cake, and whatever else came out of the oven that morning. Ask what is left.
